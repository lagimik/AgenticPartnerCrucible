# Copilot Adoption Measurement

Copilot adoption measurement starts with a decision and the evidence needed to
support it, rather than with a dashboard or a count of allocated seats.
The [Analytics Hub](../../40-Resources/analytics-hub.md) provides different tools
for readiness, usage, engagement, sentiment, and value questions.

## Practical Evidence Boundaries

- Select a report whose data source and prerequisites match the intended question.
- Compare progress with the organization's own baseline and rollout hypothesis;
  peer-company rates can hide different cultures, features, cohorts, and maturity.
- Keep usage, behavioral associations, sentiment, and estimated value distinct
  from verified business outcomes.
- Check data completeness and access before interpreting missing activity as
  lack of adoption.

The final data-completeness check is a partner interpretation of the toolkit's
report-specific prerequisites, not a universal diagnostic rule.
