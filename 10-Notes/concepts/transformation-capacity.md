# Transformation Capacity

An organization's repeatable ability to move from an approved idea to a production capability, measurable outcome, and scaled operating practice.

## What Constrains It

- Missing business context or accountable process ownership
- Data, identity, permission, security, and integration delays
- Absent evaluation, telemetry, exception handling, or escalation
- Pilots without baselines, outcome measures, adoption plans, or production funding
- Delivery models that postpone skills transfer and operating ownership

## Improvement Pattern

1. Select a measurable outcome rather than a broad technology ambition.
2. Observe real work, handoffs, exceptions, and trust boundaries.
3. Deploy the smallest production workflow that can test the operating model.
4. Measure quality, adoption, cost, exceptions, and business results together.
5. Reuse platform controls and transfer capability while scaling.
