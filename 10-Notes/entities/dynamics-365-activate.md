# Dynamics 365 Activate

An AI-assisted Dynamics 365 implementation and migration experience with
team-reviewed discovery, configuration, and migration. Salesforce CRM migration
is announced GA; ERP, including SAP ECC discovery, is private preview.
Public setup currently covers English-language US workspaces for Salesforce
to Sales or Customer Service, not unrestricted ERP access.
