# Fabric IQ

Microsoft IQ's business-context layer brings together OneLake unified data,
Power BI semantic definitions, and operational ontologies. Fabric's IQ workload
and ontology remain preview; individual connectors, agents, and consumption
experiences have distinct GA, Beta, preview, and coming-soon status.
