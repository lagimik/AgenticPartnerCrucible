# Microsoft IQ

Microsoft's November 2026 enterprise-context platform combines Work IQ,
Fabric IQ, Foundry IQ, and Web IQ. Business plugins make curated sources,
skills, tools, and business know-how reusable across supported agents.
Platform branding does not establish universal capability GA, source access,
licensing, or action authority.
