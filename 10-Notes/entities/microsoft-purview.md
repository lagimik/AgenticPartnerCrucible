# Microsoft Purview

The [November 2026 strategy announcement](../../40-Resources/microsoft-data-governance-fabric-transition.md)
retains Purview's data-security and compliance role, including classification,
labeling, protection, and DLP across Microsoft 365, Azure, Fabric, and AI.

## Governance Transition Boundary

Microsoft separately announces retirement of **Purview Data Governance**, with
support ending November 12, 2027, and positions Fabric/OneLake catalog as its
data-governance foundation. This is not an announcement that all Purview
capabilities are being retired.

Regulated and contractual cases require early Microsoft account-team assessment;
feature parity, transition tooling, and exception terms are not established by
the article.
