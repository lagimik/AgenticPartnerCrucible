# Agentic AI for Plant Operations

Microsoft Cloud manufacturing article on moving plant operations from dashboards and alerts to trusted human-agent teams that help operators decide and act in live production contexts.

## Summary

The article frames agentic AI in manufacturing as governed operational intelligence, not black-box autonomy. It emphasizes operational context, plant constraints, human approvals, trust, ontologies, and repeatable playbooks across troubleshooting, shutdown planning, reliability triage, quality response, and maintenance.

## Partner relevance

- Gives manufacturing partners a pragmatic playbook for agentic AI in brownfield environments.
- Connects operational data, engineering documents, digital twins, work orders, shift logs, and governance.
- Includes Yara and Kongsberg Digital results around faster shutdown recovery, fewer field trips, and targeted engineering efficiency gains.
