# Agentic AI Retail Economics

Microsoft Cloud retail and consumer goods article arguing that retail AI has moved from pilots to measurable economics across marketing, supply chain, and store operations.

## Summary

The article cites a Forrester TEI study projecting 124% to 282% ROI over three years for Microsoft AI solutions in retail and consumer goods. It highlights AI shopping assistants, AI-assisted campaign execution, demand forecasting, inventory optimization, exception-based planning, digital shelf labels, and frontline task automation.

- Provides retail-specific ROI and line-of-business use cases for agentic AI.
- Connects Azure AI and Copilot Studio to marketing, supply chain, and store operations outcomes.
- Useful for industry conversations focused on scaling beyond pilots into operating-model redesign.
