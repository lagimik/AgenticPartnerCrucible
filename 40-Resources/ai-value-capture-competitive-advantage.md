# AI Value Capture and Competitive Advantage

BCG frames AI as a redistribution of industry value rather than only a
productivity technology. The analysis argues that common models and inference
economics will narrow technology differentiation, moving durable advantage
toward assets that compound with use.

## Decision Framework

Five factors shape who captures value and how quickly it moves:

- Task substitutability, market concentration, and data defensibility influence
  where value accrues.
- Compute intensity and regulatory friction influence the speed and leakage of
  value transfer.
- Flow data, trusted relationships, system-level integration, network scale,
  and scarce physical assets are presented as more durable than stock data,
  headcount scale, or generic expertise.

The article warns about "hollowing": revenue and customer relationships may
remain stable while pricing power and high-margin work migrate to AI-native
competitors or infrastructure providers.

## Partner Relevance

- Lead executive workshops with value-pool and margin-shift analysis, not a
  generic AI use-case inventory.
- Identify customer assets that can compound through AI and distinguish them
  from capabilities becoming commoditized.
- Connect transformation roadmaps to business-model redesign, data flywheels,
  operating cost, and defensible customer relationships.

The figures and market forecasts are BCG estimates and should be treated as
time-bound strategic analysis rather than measured outcomes for every sector.
