# Copilot Cowork Overview

Microsoft Learn's official overview of **Microsoft Copilot Cowork**, the delegated-task agent that carries out multi-step work across Microsoft 365 on a user's behalf rather than only suggesting what could be done.

## What Cowork Does

Describe a need and Cowork executes it end to end, showing each step so the user can follow along: drafts/sends/replies to email, schedules meetings and manages calendars, creates Word/Excel/PowerPoint/PDF documents, posts in Teams channels and chats, searches across organizational resources, manages OneDrive/SharePoint files, conducts multi-source research, prepares briefings and meeting-intelligence summaries, drafts stakeholder communications, and runs prompts on a recurring schedule. Education-specific scenarios include preparing course materials and coordinating research/grant work. A Frontier-program `App` skill lets users build lightweight interactive apps from a description, with no coding required.

## Availability

Cowork is available for both personal and work/school accounts with similar feature sets: generally available for work/school accounts, in preview for personal accounts.

## Why It Matters for Partners

- Gives partners an authoritative, public reference point to use directly with customers when explaining what Cowork actually automates versus traditional Copilot chat assistance — useful at the "Inspire" stage of an envisioning engagement (see [[land-your-offer-copilot-envisioning-poc]]).
- Confirms Cowork's app-building (`/app`) skill ties directly into Copilot Credits consumption and governance, reinforcing the FinOps-for-AI conversation partners should be having alongside any Cowork rollout.
- A stable, non-gated link (unlike several companion Cowork partner assets) suitable for direct inclusion in customer-facing collateral.
