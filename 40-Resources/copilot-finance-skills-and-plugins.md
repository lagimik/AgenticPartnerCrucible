# Microsoft Copilot Finance Skills and Plugins

The [October 29 announcement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/blog/2026/10/29/microsoft-copilot-for-finance-purpose-built-for-the-way-finance-teams-work/)
describes role-specific finance workflows across Copilot in Excel and Cowork,
not one uniformly released finance product.

## Availability at Capture

| Capability | Announced status |
| --- | --- |
| Accounting capabilities in Excel and Cowork | Frontier |
| Finance skills in Copilot in Excel | Generally available |
| Capital Markets and Finance plugins in Cowork | Generally available |
| Dynamics 365 ERP apps plugin and partner plugins | Beginning registry rollout |
| Fabric IQ in Cowork | Generally available |

Do not equate this announcement with the lifecycle of the older standalone
Copilot for Finance preview. Check the specific experience, tenant enablement,
licensing, and plugin access before a pilot.

## Source Summary

Accounting scenarios include reconciliation, draft journals, and variance
commentary. Cowork scenarios assemble close packages, forecast reviews, valuation
models, and sourced investment briefs for professional review. Work IQ supplies
accessible organizational context; ERP and third-party integrations provide
additional business data subject to their own prerequisites.

The announcement describes an optional accounting audit trail in Excel chat
history, not a mandatory or independently certified audit-control system.
Third-party services may require separate subscriptions, authentication, and
administrator enablement.

**Partner interpretation:** start with a bounded close or reconciliation pilot,
validate calculations and source lineage, separate assumptions from sourced
inputs, and leave journals, recommendations, approvals, and actions under
professional control. Published customer examples are not outcome guarantees.
