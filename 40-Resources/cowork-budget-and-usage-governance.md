# Cowork Budget and Usage Governance

Microsoft Adoption's guidance for IT admins and AI leads on controlling and monitoring **Copilot Cowork** consumption once it is rolled out to users.

## Key Points

- Cowork work runs on **Copilot Credits**, the same consumption-based unit used by Copilot Code and Autopilot (see [[land-your-offer-copilot-envisioning-poc]]). Admins set organization-level budgets and per-user guardrails so Cowork spend stays predictable.
- Usage dashboards give admins visibility into credit consumption trends by user/department, so overages or runaway automation jobs can be caught early rather than discovered at the invoice.
- The guidance frames Cowork governance as an ongoing operational discipline, not a one-time licensing decision — budgets, guardrails, and usage review are expected to be revisited as adoption scales.

## Why It Matters for Partners

- Gives partners a concrete, public talking point for the "FinOps for AI" conversation that increasingly accompanies any Copilot Cowork or agent-consumption deployment — directly reinforces the envisioning-stage messaging already captured in [[land-your-offer-copilot-envisioning-poc]].
- A natural companion to the [[copilot-cowork-overview]] page: pair "what Cowork does" with "how to keep its spend under control" in the same customer conversation.
- Supports a managed-service upsell: ongoing budget/guardrail tuning and usage review is recurring, billable practice work, not a one-time setup task.
