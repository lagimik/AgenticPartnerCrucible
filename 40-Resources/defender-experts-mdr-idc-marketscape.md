# Defender Experts MDR in the 2026 IDC MarketScape

Microsoft announcement that it was named a Leader in the 2026 IDC MarketScape for worldwide enterprise MDR/MXDR.

## Summary

Microsoft Defender Experts MDR is an expert-led, round-the-clock managed detection and response service operating natively on Microsoft Defender across endpoints, identities, email, cloud apps, cloud workloads, and network security. Proactive threat hunting is included through Defender Experts Hunting.

The service combines AI-assisted noise filtering, grading, classification, investigation, and agentic operations with human experts who retain decision ownership. Microsoft also emphasizes continuous feedback from service investigations into the broader Defender platform.

## Partner relevance

- Provides a benchmark for expert-led MDR service design on the Defender platform.
- Reinforces the value of combining platform telemetry, threat intelligence, automation, hunting, and human accountability.
- Supports partner conversations about extending understaffed SOC teams and differentiating managed security services.

The performance figures and market-position claims are quoted by Microsoft from the IDC assessment and Microsoft service operations; customers should review the report excerpt and service scope.
