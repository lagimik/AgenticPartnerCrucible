## Summary

The Cloud Adoption Framework's executive strategy guidance treats digital sovereignty as a set of workload and organizational requirements, not a single cloud deployment choice. It directs leaders to define the controls they need, assess whether standard cloud capabilities meet them, and select stronger operational or infrastructure controls when specific requirements call for them.

## Partner Relevance

Use the guidance to structure a sovereignty-planning engagement:

- Identify applicable legal, regulatory, data, access, and operational requirements by workload.
- Separate mandatory controls from preferences, and record how each will be verified.
- Select an operating and deployment model that satisfies those requirements without assuming every workload needs the same level of isolation.
- Plan governance, skills, shared responsibilities, and ongoing review as requirements change.

Use the framework as planning guidance, not as legal advice or a substitute for customer-specific compliance review.
