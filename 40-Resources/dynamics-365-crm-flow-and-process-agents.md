# Dynamics 365 CRM in the Flow of Work and Process Agents

Microsoft describes moving CRM context and actions into Copilot and Teams while agents carry defined sales and service processes across systems, involving people for review, judgment, and specialist input.

## Announced Capabilities

- Thirty prebuilt Dynamics 365 Sales, Customer Service, and Customer Insights skills are generally available in Copilot Cowork. Autopilot is in private preview; Code is available through the Frontier Program.
- Dynamics 365 CRM in Teams is in public preview, bringing sales and service account context, meeting briefs, case notifications, and account developments into connected channels.
- Service Agent in Microsoft 365 is being extended to select Frontier customers in private preview. Microsoft's example includes drafting a response for approval, creating a CRM case when required, and escalating issues to a specialist.
- Sales Development Agent is in public preview for prospect research, personalized outreach, follow-up, and qualification before seller handoff. The article states that it will begin consuming Copilot credits on 2026-11-16.

These availability statements reflect the Microsoft article published 2026-10-08. Confirm current product documentation and eligibility before planning a deployment.

## Partner Relevance

Use the announcements to frame CRM and agent-workflow discovery: map customer context to the places users collaborate, identify actions that require human review, define cross-system process boundaries and escalation paths, and account for permissions, governance, preview eligibility, and consumption.

The article describes product direction and capabilities; it does not establish independent performance results or guarantee customer outcomes.
