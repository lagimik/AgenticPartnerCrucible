# Dynamics 365 in the 2026 CRM Sales Platforms Magic Quadrant

Microsoft announcement that it was recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for CRM Sales Platforms, marking its sixteenth consecutive year in the Leaders quadrant.

## Summary

The article frames modern CRM as a system of action rather than only a system of record. Dynamics 365 Sales combines CRM, productivity, collaboration, analytics, workflow automation, and extensible agents to help sellers synthesize context, prioritize work, and execute next steps.

Highlighted capabilities include Sales Research Agent, Data Enrichment Agent, Sales Agent, and Sales Qualification Agent. Sales Agent in Microsoft 365 Copilot brings meeting preparation, record summaries, follow-up assistance, and sales insights into Outlook and Teams. Copilot Studio and Power Platform support customer-specific agents and workflows.

## Partner relevance

- Supports CRM modernization engagements focused on seller action and decision support.
- Connects Dynamics 365 Sales with Microsoft 365 Copilot and Power Platform.
- Creates implementation opportunities around data quality, workflow automation, governance, and specialized sales agents.

The market-position claim is from a Microsoft-authored summary; customers should evaluate the full analyst report and their own requirements.
