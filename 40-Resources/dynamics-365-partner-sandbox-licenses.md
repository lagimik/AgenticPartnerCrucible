# Dynamics 365 Partner Sandbox Licenses

Microsoft partner resources for understanding and obtaining Dynamics 365 sandbox licenses. The overview datasheet introduces the offer, while the how-to guide provides the operational steps for partners.

## Resources

- [Partner Sandbox Licenses overview](https://aka.ms/sandboxoverview) - Offer datasheet.
- [Partner Sandbox Licenses how-to guide](https://aka.ms/sandboxhowto) - Partner setup and acquisition guidance.

Both downloads are hosted on the Microsoft Partners portal and may require an authenticated partner account.

## Partner Relevance

- Establish non-production Dynamics 365 environments for learning, testing, demonstrations, and solution development.
- Use the overview to identify the offer before following the acquisition guide.
- Pair with discounted non-production license options when planning a Business Applications demo environment.
