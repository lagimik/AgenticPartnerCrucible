# Hosters Marketing Moment

Microsoft Partner blog article for hosting and hybrid cloud partners moving from awareness of infrastructure market shifts to practical modernization action with Microsoft Adaptive Cloud.

## Summary

The article frames hosting modernization as an evolution without disruption: preserve existing customer trust and operational control, modernize platform foundations where it matters, and grow into higher-value managed services across Azure, hybrid, and AI-ready scenarios.

It points partners to the `Navigating the Next Era of Hosting` e-book and the Microsoft Datacenter Optimization (DCO) initiative as practical resources for planning the transition.

## Phased modernization path

| Phase | Partner motion |
| --- | --- |
| Stabilize and build customer confidence | Maintain trust, workload placement, and operational control while improving visibility, policy, and governance across current environments. |
| Modernize the platform at your pace | Use Azure Arc for a unified operating layer, Azure Local for Azure-integrated partner-operated infrastructure, and Azure Native services for security, resilience, data, and application capabilities. |
| Differentiate, expand, and grow | Shift from capacity-led hosting toward recurring managed services around governance, security, monitoring, cost optimization, modernization, and AI-ready workloads. |

## Partner relevance

- Gives hosters a customer continuity narrative for licensing, cost, and platform-change conversations.
- Connects Azure Arc, Azure Local, and Azure Native services into one Adaptive Cloud modernization motion.
- Positions DCO as the sustained resource hub for partner guidance, tooling, engineering engagement, and next steps.
- Bridges SPLA-era hosting toward CSP services-led revenue and managed services.
