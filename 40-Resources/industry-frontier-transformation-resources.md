# Industry Frontier Transformation Resources

Microsoft partner collection for industry-focused Frontier Transformation assets.

## Use

Use this collection as a starting point for industry-specific customer conversations, transformation scenarios, and partner enablement material. The short link currently resolves to the Microsoft partner asset collection titled **Explore Industry Frontier Transformation Resources**.

## Access Note

The public landing page exposes limited descriptive content and may require Partner Center authentication for the full collection. Treat the collection contents as current portal material rather than a fixed catalog.
