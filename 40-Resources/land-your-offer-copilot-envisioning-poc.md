# Land Your Offer - Copilot Envisioning & POC (Part 2 of Partner Offer Anatomy Series)

Microsoft Partner Community Blog walkthrough (2026-10-01, Part 2 of the
"anatomy of a revenue-generating partner offer" series — see
[[land-your-offer-copilot-in-30]] for Part 1) for the **Frontier
Accelerate for Copilot: Envisioning & POC** engagement: a Microsoft-funded,
partner-led, pre-sales offer for customers with 300+ Office 365/Microsoft
365 seats, sized across six tiers (XXS 10 hrs to XXL 280 hrs) through five
phases (Assess, Inspire, Design, POC, Executive Summary). It lands
alongside Microsoft's new usage-based Copilot layer — **Cowork, Code, and
Autopilot** — governed through FinOps for AI, making the envisioning
engagement the front door to that bigger consumption-based conversation.
Includes a full hours-by-tier activity table and a worked revenue model
stacking MCI engagement incentives, CSP incentives on resulting licenses,
and a detailed, outcome-priced managed-services catalog for after the
executive readout.

## Why It Matters for Partners

- A Microsoft-funded (not free-to-the-partner) pre-sales offer template
  for the enterprise/mid-market segment, directly complementing the SMB
  Copilot in 30 offer from Part 1 — together covering the full customer
  size spectrum with a repeatable, publishable Marketplace structure.
- Names the exact tiering mechanics (seat thresholds, hour sizing, MCI
  funding amounts per tier) partners need to scope and price an
  envisioning engagement without guessing, and to pick the right tier for
  a given account.
- Surfaces the new Cowork/Code/Autopilot usage-based billing layer and
  FinOps for AI governance need as a fresh, high-relevance conversation
  starter — positions the partner to lead consumption-model education
  before competitors do.
- Provides a detailed, outcome-priced managed-services catalog (security
  and governance, adoption and change, agent build-out, FinOps for AI
  consumption/license management, quarterly value reviews) with
  illustrative effort-in-days and measurable customer outcomes per
  service — directly reusable as line items in a partner's own managed
  services pricing sheet.
- Reinforces the Part 1 pattern that the executive readout/conversion
  point is the start, not the end, of revenue — with concrete figures
  partners can adapt for their own ROI storytelling (e.g., time released
  vs. seat cost, cost-per-outcome trending).
- Explicit caveat: all dollar figures are illustrative estimates from the
  offer plan — confirm current MCI payouts, CSP rates, eligibility, and
  proof-of-execution requirements in the Microsoft Commercial Partner
  Incentives Guide before quoting to a customer.
