# Microsoft AI Coach for Personalized Sales Training

Inside Track article on Agent J.ai, an internal Microsoft AI-powered coach modeled on commercial CEO Judson Althoff to help sellers practice customer and partner conversations at scale.

## Summary

Agent J.ai shifts skilling from static training to scenario-driven coaching. It lets customer-facing employees practice executive conversations, prepare for customer meetings, and receive contextual guidance grounded in curated content, Microsoft customer engagement frameworks, and current announcements.

## Partner relevance

- Demonstrates a Customer Zero pattern for using AI to scale role-specific sales enablement.
- Gives partners a model for building coaching agents that support account planning, opportunity qualification, and customer meeting preparation.
- Highlights voice-first subject-matter-expert agents and privacy-conscious learning experiences.
