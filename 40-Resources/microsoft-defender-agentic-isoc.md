# Microsoft Defender Agentic ISOC

Microsoft announced the integrated security operations center (ISOC) in
Microsoft Defender as a preview foundation that brings SIEM and native threat
protection into one system for people and agents.

## Operating Model

- Signals and sensors provide awareness.
- Context turns signals into operational understanding.
- Actuators translate decisions into protection.
- Agents provide continuous speed and scale while people set strategy,
  priorities, judgment, and accountability.
- An integrated protection loop uses telemetry, exposure insights, threat
  intelligence, and controls to strengthen pre-breach protection and disrupt
  attacks in progress.

The intended change is from practitioners stitching together tools and context
to organizing work around security outcomes in a shared Defender foundation.

## Partner Relevance

Security partners can assess readiness for integrated operations, consolidate
SIEM and threat-protection workflows, engineer signals and context, define
human-agent decision boundaries, and modernize SOC processes. ISOC was
announced in preview, so production scope and availability must be validated.
