# Microsoft FinOps for AI Capabilities

Microsoft positions FinOps for AI as a shared discipline across finance, IT,
business leaders, developers, makers, and end users. The objective is to
control spend while connecting consumption to business value.

## Current Foundation

Cost management in Agent 365 provides centralized controls for usage-based
billed services:

- Usage-based services are off by default.
- Spending policies can be scoped at tenant, group, and user levels.
- Alerts can be configured against policy thresholds.
- Azure subscriptions and resource groups support departmental accountability.
- Reporting can break usage down by users, groups, workloads, policies, and
  payment methods.

The source also announces additional capabilities across Agent 365, Insights,
and Microsoft Copilot, with availability varying by feature.

## Partner Relevance

Partners can package AI FinOps readiness, billing-policy design, cost
allocation, usage reporting, forecasting, value measurement, and continuous
optimization. Availability and licensing must be verified before proposals
because the announcement mixes capabilities available now with capabilities
coming later.
