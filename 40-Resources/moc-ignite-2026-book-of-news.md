# Microsoft Ignite 2026 Book of News Map

An event-specific map following the Book of News's eight topic areas and 23
category headings, in source order. The linked pages cover 59 announcement
entries with paraphrased titles and summaries. Existing product and concept
pages remain the durable knowledge layer; this map does not reorganize the
whole vault or replace its taxonomy.

## Introduction

The source is an announcement guide, not a deployment specification. Its event
dates are November 17-20, 2026. The [source capture](../raw/2026-11-27-ignite-2026-book-of-news/source.md)
records provenance and limitations. Distinguish GA, preview, private preview,
Frontier, pilot and announced future rollout for each capability.
