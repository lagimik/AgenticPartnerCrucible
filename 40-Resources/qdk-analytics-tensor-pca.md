# QDK Analytics and Quantum Tensor PCA

Microsoft QDK for analytics is entering private preview as a research toolkit
for evaluating quantum approaches to complex, high-dimensional data.

## Capabilities

- Modular data representations and quantum primitives.
- Integration with familiar data preparation, evaluation, and visualization
  workflows.
- Classical baselines and statistical tools for reproducibility and comparison.
- Quantum Tensor PCA research aimed at revealing higher-order correlations.

Microsoft and LSEG plan to evaluate candidate financial scenarios including
mortgage prepayment, credit and liquidity risk, counterparty risk and XVA,
cross-asset tail risk, fixed-income analytics, ESG risk, and market-data
reliability.

## Partner Relevance

The immediate opportunity is discovery and research enablement rather than
production quantum transformation. Partners can help customers select
high-dimensional problems, establish strong classical baselines, define
measurable success criteria, prepare governed datasets, and evaluate whether
quantum methods add evidence-based value.

Claims of potential speedup are research claims and scenario-dependent. The
private preview should not be represented as production readiness.
