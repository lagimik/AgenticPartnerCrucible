# Responsible AI in 2026

Microsoft's third annual Responsible AI Transparency Report frames trust as an operational, lifecycle discipline for increasingly agentic systems.

## Three Investment Areas

1. Adaptive governance and technical risk management aligned to models, platform services, applications, and Microsoft's role in the AI value chain.
2. Practical tools for evaluation, red teaming, policy controls, observability, and intervention.
3. Shared research, standards, partnerships, transparency, and comparable benchmarks.

Agentic systems expand governance beyond a model's outputs to identity, memory, tools, permissions, data, actions, and interactions among agents and people. The resulting control loop is continuous: evaluate, observe, enforce, learn, and update.
