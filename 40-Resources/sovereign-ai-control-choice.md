## Summary

Microsoft's October 2026 blog and companion whitepaper frame sovereign AI around control, choice, flexibility, and resilience. They position sovereignty as broader than data residency: organizations also need to consider access, model and infrastructure choices, governance, and how workloads operate when connectivity or other dependencies change.

The materials describe a range of cloud and local deployment choices, including Azure Local and disconnected operation for workloads with stricter control or continuity requirements. They do not prescribe one deployment model for every workload.

## Partner Relevance

Start with workload-specific requirements: data and processing location, administrator and operator access, encryption control, model options, connectivity assumptions, and continuity needs. Map those requirements to a deployment and operating model, then document shared responsibilities, governance, and evidence needed to verify controls.

These are Microsoft-authored materials and an architectural framing, not independent certification or a determination that a particular design satisfies a customer's legal obligations.
