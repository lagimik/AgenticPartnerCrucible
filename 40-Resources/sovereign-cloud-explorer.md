# Microsoft Sovereign Cloud Explorer

## Source Summary

The public [Sovereign Cloud Explorer](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/sovereignty/sovereign-cloud-explorer/)
offers nine questions to recommend a sovereignty starting point. Its embedded
assessment covers policy and audit drivers, jurisdiction, certification,
workload and AI scope, operator access, residency and confidentiality,
resilience, and market-specific operating requirements.

The page's public configuration was reviewed; no organizational answers were
submitted and no recommendation or deployment was validated.

## Partner Application

**Interpretation:** Use the explorer as a discovery-workshop aid, then turn the
answers into workload-specific requirements, evidence requests, and architecture
options. Distinguish data at rest from processing, metadata, logs, backups,
support access, key custody, and disconnected continuity.

An interactive recommendation is neither a compliance certification nor legal
advice. Validate requirements with accountable customer owners and current
service documentation before selecting an operating model.
