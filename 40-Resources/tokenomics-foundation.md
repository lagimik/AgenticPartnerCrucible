# Tokenomics Foundation

Public practitioner channel describing a vendor-neutral discipline for connecting AI production and consumption to total cost, quality, and measurable business value.

## Useful Themes

- **Total Cost of AI:** Include infrastructure, energy, software, labor, review, governance, and process effects rather than treating API charges as the whole bill.
- **Routing and consumption:** Evaluate model routing, gateways, caching, metering, forecasting, and quality thresholds together.
- **Value architecture:** Define baselines, counterfactuals, acceptable quality, measurable outcomes, and where value appears in operating or financial reporting.
- **Pilot governance:** Time-box experiments, state the value hypothesis, define stop/go criteria, and assign accountable owners.
- **Consumption visibility:** Consolidate usage across internal applications, SaaS products, cloud AI services, and external APIs.

## Evidence Boundary

The discipline and definitions are emerging. Creator-supplied percentages, survey correlations, and market claims require independent validation before use in customer business cases. Link to the original public videos rather than reproducing audiovisual content or transcripts.
