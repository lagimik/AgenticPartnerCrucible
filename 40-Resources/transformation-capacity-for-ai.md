# Transformation Capacity for AI

Telecommunications-focused operating-model guidance for repeatedly moving AI ideas into production workflows with measurable outcomes and durable customer capability.

## Diagnostic Questions

- How long does an approved use case take to reach production?
- Where do permissions, data access, security review, integration, observability, exceptions, or ownership stall delivery?
- Is the initiative tied to a baseline, target outcome, process owner, and adoption plan?
- Can the smallest useful workflow be deployed and measured in a short cycle?
- Are reusable identity, evaluation, approval, telemetry, cost, and incident controls available?
- Does the engagement transfer architecture, runbooks, and operating ownership to the customer?

## Delivery Pattern

1. Begin with a measurable operational outcome.
2. Observe how frontline work and exceptions actually occur.
3. Deploy the smallest production workflow that proves the operating model.
4. Close learning loops for quality, adoption, exceptions, oversight, cost, and business results.
5. Reuse platform controls and transfer capability while scaling.
