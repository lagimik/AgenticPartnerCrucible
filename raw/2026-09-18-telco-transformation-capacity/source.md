## Direct Source Summary

The article argues that telecommunications operators often have ample AI ideas, pilots, models, infrastructure, and engineering expertise but insufficient capacity to move repeatedly from idea to production, measurable outcome, and scaled capability. It calls that organizational ability transformation capacity.

The source says AI initiatives commonly fail at operational seams such as missing business context, permissions, observability, exception handling, escalation, governance, and cross-organizational trust. It proposes an outcome-first build-with model: observe real work, deploy the smallest production workflow, use short engineering cycles, close learning loops, and deliberately transfer capability to customer teams.

The modernization framing is "migrate, modernize, agentify." Microsoft Frontier Company is described as an embedded-engineering model rather than a product or permanent outsourcing arrangement.

## Interpretation

Partners can assess pilot-to-production cycle time, rescue stalled pilots, rationalize use-case portfolios, create reusable agent platform controls, add agentic redesign to modernization roadmaps, and make capability transfer an explicit delivery outcome.
