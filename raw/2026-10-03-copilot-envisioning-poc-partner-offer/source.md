## Premise

Where Part 1 (Copilot in 30) covered a $0 SMB trial offer, Part 2 covers a
larger, **funded** (not free) engagement for enterprise/mid-market
customers: the **Frontier Accelerate for Copilot: Envisioning & POC**
offer. It lands alongside Microsoft's newly introduced "Copilot" layer —
**Cowork, Code, and Autopilot** — which run on usage-based billing (rather
than per-user subscription) and are governed through FinOps for AI,
making a structured envisioning engagement the front door to a bigger,
consumption-based conversation.

## The enterprise opportunity

Target customers have 300+ Office 365/Microsoft 365 seats (mid-market
through largest enterprises). They already believe Copilot and agents
matter but lack: where AI will pay off, what readiness requires, and how
to prove value before committing budget. This ambition-to-plan gap is
where partners win — the envisioning stage is where the partner shapes
the roadmap, scope, and commercial conversation before anyone else does.

## Why Copilot + agents, and the new "Copilot" layer

- Microsoft 365 Copilot: personal productivity inside Outlook, Teams,
  Word, Excel, PowerPoint — value without a platform change.
- Agent 365, custom Copilot Studio agents, and Microsoft 365 E7: extend
  Copilot into role-based and process-level automation for larger orgs.
- The new third layer: **Cowork** (delegated work returning a finished
  result), **Code** (natural-language app/dashboard/automation building,
  hosted on Copilot Managed Runtime), and **Autopilot** (a persistent
  agent with its own identity). All three run on **usage-based billing**,
  meaning every customer must decide which users/scenarios justify
  consumption spend and how to govern it with FinOps for AI.

## The offer: Envisioning & POC, sized to the customer

A pre-sales, partner-led engagement identifying personas/use cases and
building a business case for Microsoft 365 Copilot, Agent 365, Microsoft
365 E7, and/or agents, with an optional POC.

- **Eligibility:** minimum 300 O365/M365 seats; larger tiers unlock at
  500, 1,000, 1,500, 3,000/5,000, and 10,000+ seats.
- **Sizing — six tiers:** XXS (10 hrs), XS (20 hrs), S (40 hrs), M (100
  hrs), L/XL (150 hrs), XXL (280 hrs) — hours scale with seats in scope.
- **Five phases:** Assess, Inspire, Design, POC, Executive Summary — with
  a working POC and measured results from the S tier upward.
- **Microsoft provides:** delivery guide, pre-engagement planning
  resources, and Microsoft Commercial Incentives (MCI) funding tied to
  proof of execution.
- **Customer gets:** readiness findings, a business case and value plan
  (including usage-based billing and Copilot Credits), technical
  requirements and a remediation plan, an adoption roadmap, a working POC,
  and an executive readout.

Microsoft funds the engagement; the partner owns scope, relationship, and
everything that follows.

## Five-phase delivery model

1. **Assess** — identify high-value scenarios, select assessments, define
   success, deliver readiness findings.
2. **Inspire** — product overviews/demos across Copilot and agent
   capabilities; make security/governance, adoption/change, and
   reporting/analytics concrete; build a stories/scenario library.
3. **Design** — business case/value plan (incl. usage-based billing),
   remediation plan, technical/functional requirements, adoption roadmap,
   POC success criteria and environment prep, recommendations/risks/
   owners/dates.
4. **POC** — scope users/products/features, confirm requirements/design,
   IT access and environment prep, trial licenses, build the POC agent(s)
   /solution(s) (confirming Copilot Credits consumption), train POC
   users, measure/evaluate results (usage + credits consumed).
5. **Executive Summary** — executive readout on seat growth and
   consumption implications, then convert: trial-to-paid licenses (T1),
   commercial proposal/signature (T2), and a named wave‑2 expansion plan
   (X1).

Total engagement hours by tier: XXS 10, XS 20, S 40, M 100, L/XL 150,
XXL 280.

Three streams stack: (1) **MCI engagement incentive** funding the
envisioning/POC work itself, paid against proof of execution; (2) **CSP
incentive** on the licenses the business case unlocks (trial-to-paid
through wave-2 expansion, growing as agent consumption/Copilot Credits
scale); (3) **managed services and follow-on professional services** —
the largest, most durable revenue share (see "After the Readout" below).

Partner Center's growth-insights reporting (ASPX dashboard) surfaces
account-level Copilot eligibility, seat whitespace, E7 opportunity, data
security maturity, MCI eligibility, and potential earnings across a
partner's base, to prioritize which qualifying customers to approach
first.

## After the readout: managed services (illustrative 1,500-seat/M-tier figures)

Five recurring, outcome-priced services, each with partner effort, what's
measured, and representative customer outcomes:

- **Deployment and remediation delivery** (5–10 partner days/wave) — close
  remediation/technical-requirement items against dated owners, license
  and activate users by roadmap date. Target: wave 1 live within 30 days
  of readout; ≥95% of paid seats active (75 idle seats ≈ $27K/yr wasted);
  rollout within ±5% of business-case budget.
- **Security and governance management** (2 days/month) — maintain
  admin-settings baseline and data-protection controls, review agent
  permissions/access dependencies, govern plugins and Copilot Managed
  Runtime apps. Target: zero oversharing incidents (avg. breach cost
  $4M+); 100% recommended settings on; findings closed within 30 days;
  Data Security Maturity at Advanced-Healthy.
- **Adoption and change management** (3–4 days/month) — run the adoption
  roadmap: champion coaching, role-based training, scenario refresh,
  monthly Copilot Analytics review. Target: ≥80% of assigned seats active
  monthly; 2–4 hrs/user/week saved (≈3,000–6,000 hrs across 1,500 seats);
  ≈$0.5–1M/month of staff time released at $40/hr.
- **Agent and solution build-out** (5–15 days/agent, then ~0.5 day/month
  to tune) — take POC agents into production via Copilot Studio/Agent
  365; release new Copilot Studio agents, Code-built apps, and Autopilot
  agents from the scenario backlog. Target: 20–40% of tier-1 tickets/cases
  deflected per automated process; cycle time cut 30–50%; cost per
  completed task tracked and falling quarter over quarter.
- **FinOps for AI: consumption and license management** (1–2 days/month)
  — track Copilot Credits/usage-based billing across Cowork, Code, and
  Autopilot; set budgets/limits/model-family policies per user group;
  route credit requests through approval workflow; forecast spend; align
  seat additions/renewals to each wave. Target: AI spend within ±10% of
  forecast; zero unused seats at renewal; cost per outcome down 10–20%/yr.
- **Quarterly value reviews** (2 days/quarter) — refresh the business case
  with actuals, capture proof points in the customer's own words, agree
  the next named expansion wave with the sponsor. Illustrative ROI:
  ≈$125K/yr of Copilot seats vs. $6M+ of time released; ≥3 quantified
  proof points/quarter; each review dates the next wave (+100–300 seats or
  +1–2 agents/quarter).

Added together, one M-tier (1,500+ seat) customer with three agents in
production sustains roughly 9–10 partner days/month after the readout,
before wave deployments and new builds are counted.
