## Premise

"Copilot in 30" is a ready-made Microsoft Marketplace/Partner Center offer
template: a $0, 25-user, 30-day Microsoft 365 Copilot Business trial,
wrapped in a structured partner-led journey, aimed at SMB customers (≤300
users on Microsoft 365 Business Basic/Standard/Premium, no paid Copilot
today). The post frames this as Part 1 of a series on the "anatomy" of a
revenue-generating partner offer — i.e., how to package a trial into a
durable, repeatable, sellable motion rather than a one-off project.

## Why Marketplace publication matters

A published Microsoft Marketplace offer gives a partner practice a
permanent, discoverable storefront where customers already look for
Copilot help. It also converts expertise into something repeatable:
instead of scoping every engagement from scratch, the partner defines the
journey once (activities, timeline, deliverables) and runs it across many
customers. Partner Center supports both professional-service and
managed-service offer types under one umbrella, so a single offer can
carry the 30-day trial journey and the managed-service follow-on.

## The SMB opportunity

SMB customers (≤300 Microsoft 365 users, Business Basic/Standard/Premium,
typically no in-house AI team) represent high demand paired with limited
internal capacity — exactly where a repeatable, partner-led offer scales
further than bespoke projects.

## Copilot in 30: the trial mechanics

- **What the customer gets:** 25 Microsoft 365 Copilot Business seats for
  30 days at $0, transacted through CSP New Commerce (Product ID
  CFQ7TTC0MM8R · SKU 006Z).
- **Eligibility:** Microsoft 365 Business Basic/Standard/Premium, no paid
  Copilot today, one trial per customer.
- **Availability:** Transactable through 31 December 2026.
- **Day 30 behavior:** Trial auto-converts to a paid subscription unless
  renewal settings are changed, with a 7-day cancellation window.
- **What Microsoft supplies:** Launch kit, campaign materials, setup
  guidance, Copilot Success Planner, and conversion guidance. Microsoft
  supplies licenses and assets; the partner owns the offer and the
  customer relationship.

## The five-stage activity plan (Identify → Plan → Activate → Experience → Convert)

- **Identify (pre-trial):** Build a prioritized target list (via the
  ASPX/Cloud Ascent dashboard), confirm eligibility, launch an acquisition
  campaign, and have the partner become "Customer Zero" by completing
  internal microskilling first.
- **Plan (pre-trial):** Confirm a named sponsor and Copilot admin, build a
  30-day success plan with agreed success measures, and confirm technical
  and compliance readiness.
- **Activate (Day 0):** Transact the trial in CSP New Commerce, configure
  paid renewal settings (quantity, term, billing), complete admin setup
  and assign all 25 licenses, run the kick-off with starter prompts.
- **Experience (Days 1–28), one weekly scenario per week:** Week 1
  Outlook, Week 2 Teams (with a Copilot Analytics checkpoint and
  re-engagement of low-activity users), Week 3 Apps, Week 4 Agents —
  capturing proof points in the customer's own words along the way.
- **Convert (Days 29–30):** Outcome/ROI review, confirm the paid offer
  (targeting a 50-seat conversion), and open the wave-2 expansion and
  consumption conversation.

## Revenue anatomy of one engagement (illustrative figures — confirm current rates in Partner Center)

| Item | Value | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trial offer | 25 seats | M365 Copilot Business, 30 days, $0, one per customer, to 31 Dec 2026 |
| CSP incentive — 25 seats | ~$0.32K | 5.0% direct bill (2.5% M365 CSP Core + 2.5% Strategic Product Accelerator Tier 1) on 25 seats × $21/mo ≈ $6.3K/yr; indirect reseller 2.5% ≈ $0.16K |
| Conversion target | 50 seats | Lead conversion with 50 paid seats; sets up the wave-2 expansion plan |
| CSP incentive — 50 seats | ~$0.63K | Same structure on 50 seats × $21/mo ≈ $12.6K/yr; indirect reseller 2.5% ≈ $0.32K |
| Frontier Accelerate deployment funding | $2.5K | Microsoft Commercial Incentives funding for the deployment engagement when conversion lands with 50 paid seats; funds deployment/adoption work leading into managed services |

Three revenue streams stack: (1) CSP incentive on every paid seat, growing
again at the 25→50 expansion; (2) Frontier Accelerate deployment funding
($2,500) when the conversion is led with 50 paid seats; (3) managed
services — the recurring, largest and most durable revenue share,
described under "Day 31 and Beyond."

Partner Center's growth-insights reporting (via the AI Business Solutions
& Security Insights / ASPX dashboard) gives account-level Copilot
eligibility, seat whitespace, free Copilot Chat usage, and adoption
signals across a partner's existing managed accounts — used to rank and
prioritize which customers to approach first (the post references a
colleague's companion Power BI dashboard that scores customers 0–100 on
eligible seats, whitespace, free-to-paid potential, and opportunity
signals).

## Day 31 and beyond: managed services that keep delivering

Framed as standing, recurring services (not one-off projects) to package
alongside the trial offer:
- Copilot adoption management (monthly Copilot Analytics business
  reviews, prompt/scenario refreshes, champion program, new-user
  onboarding).
- License and expansion management (25→50→wave‑2 seat growth; renewals,
  terms, billing).
- Agent build-out (role-based agents via Copilot Studio for sales,
  service, finance, operations — surfaced in Week 4 of the trial).
- Copilot Cowork enablement and governance (consumption-based scenarios,
  budgets, cost controls, monthly usage reporting).
- Security, compliance, and readiness (data protection, permissions, and
  governance kept in step with expanding AI use, including a path to
  Microsoft 365 Business Premium).
- Quarterly value reviews (refresh success measures, capture new proof
  points, agree the next expansion plan with the sponsor).
