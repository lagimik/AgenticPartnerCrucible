# Source Summary: Dynamics 365 CRM in the Flow of Work and Business Process Agents

- Source: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/blog/2026/10/08/bringing-crm-into-the-flow-of-work-and-agents-into-business-process-for-sales-and-service-teams/
- Publisher: Microsoft Copilot Blog
- Published: 2026-10-08
- Reviewed and captured: 2026-10-09

## Summary

Microsoft describes two related directions for sales and service: bring CRM context and actions into Copilot and Teams, and use agents to advance defined business processes across teams and systems, involving people where judgment, approval, or expertise is needed.

The article covers 30 prebuilt Dynamics 365 Sales, Customer Service, and Customer Insights skills, generally available in Copilot Cowork. Autopilot is in private preview, and Code is available through the Frontier Program. Dynamics 365 CRM in Teams is in public preview and can surface account context, meeting briefs, case notifications, and account developments in connected channels.

It also describes preview agents for process work. Service Agent in Microsoft 365 is being extended to select Frontier customers in private preview; its example drafts a response for review, creates a CRM case when needed, and escalates to specialists. Sales Development Agent is in public preview and researches prospects, personalizes outreach, follows up, and qualifies opportunities for seller handoff. The article says it will start consuming Copilot credits on 2026-11-16.

## Partner Relevance

Use these announcements to scope CRM modernization and agent-workflow discovery: identify where users need customer context, which actions remain subject to human review, what cross-system processes could be delegated, and how previews, identity, permissions, governance, and consumption affect readiness. Confirm current availability and product requirements before proposing a deployment.

## Evidence Boundaries

This summary reports Microsoft-published product announcements and availability labels as stated in the article on its publication date. It does not independently verify customer outcomes or imply that every capability is generally available.
