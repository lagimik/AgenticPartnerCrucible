# Source Capture

Paraphrased summary of the retrieved public announcement, not a full-text
reproduction. The page shows November 12 publication and an earlier November 11
update label; the discrepancy is preserved without correcting the source.

## Source Summary

Microsoft identifies Fabric and the OneLake catalog as its data-governance
foundation. Governance is positioned alongside data use, with policies,
lineage, stewardship, trust signals, and integration through supported shortcuts,
mirroring, and connectors.

The announcement states that Purview Data Governance will be retired and support
will end November 12, 2027. Microsoft says it is supporting affected customers
in transitioning to Fabric.

This is not retirement of all Microsoft Purview: the announcement retains
Purview's data-security and compliance role, including classification, labeling,
protection, DLP, and compliance capabilities integrated with Microsoft 365,
Azure, and Fabric.

Microsoft says governance is included in Fabric capacity instead of separate
meters. It also acknowledges requirements beyond current Fabric capabilities
and names integrations with Atlan, Alation, Collibra, and Informatica.

## Customer Guidance

Customers with contractual obligations or in highly regulated industries should
engage their Microsoft account teams early for individual assessment. The source
says detailed transition guidance is available, but does not link that guidance
directly or enumerate migration steps, feature parity, or exception terms.

## Evidence Boundaries

This is a strategy and support-end announcement, not proof of automatic
migration, universal connector coverage, regulatory compliance, or cost savings.
The linked catalog article and roadmap were not independently reviewed.
Capacity inclusion does not establish unlimited usage or zero total cost.
No customer transition, resource change, or Purview policy change was performed.
